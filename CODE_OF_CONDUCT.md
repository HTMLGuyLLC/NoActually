# Code of Conduct

No, Actually... is about catching mistakes, so this project has a lot of practice at being wrong in public. Do it kindly.

## Expected

- Assume good intent. Correct the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem obvious.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's chats, and redact your own before sharing an example.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information, including chat logs, without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or a private security advisory. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may ban contributors who break this code, temporarily or permanently.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It's adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
